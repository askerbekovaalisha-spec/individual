Task 10. Mouse and focus events

Hover or focus this card

Move the mouse over the card or press Tab to focus it.

  • mouseenter — adds the highlight and changes the instruction (mouse).
  • mouseleave — removes the highlight and restores the text (mouse).
  • focus — the same as mouseenter, but for the keyboard (Tab).
  • blur — the same as mouseleave, when the card loses focus.
